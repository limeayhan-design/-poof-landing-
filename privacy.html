Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

Overview

Poof is a file-sharing app designed to work across devices and operating systems. We built Poof with privacy as a first-class concern. This page explains what data we collect, why, how we store it, and your rights.

Data we collect

Account data (only if you sign in)

  • Apple ID user identifier — a stable anonymous ID Apple provides when you use Sign in with Apple. We use it to link your Premium entitlement across your devices.
  • Name and email — only if you choose to share them via Sign in with Apple. Used to personalise your Poof profile and, if you opted in via our waitlist, to send you your redemption code.

Device data (for pairing)

  • A device identifier we generate locally (not tied to your Apple ID). Used to route file transfers between your devices.
  • Optional device name you choose (e.g. "Uras's iPhone").

Transfer metadata

  • File names, file sizes, and transfer timestamps — kept temporarily to route the transfer and expire automatically within 24 hours.
  • File contents are never inspected, indexed, or retained. Files pass through our relay only when a direct peer-to-peer connection is not possible.

Diagnostic data

  • Anonymous crash reports and performance metrics via Apple's built-in analytics (only if you opted in at the system level).

Data we do not collect

  • The contents of any file you send or receive.
  • Contact lists, message history, browsing history, or location.
  • Any advertising identifier. Poof shows no ads.

Third-party services

Poof relies on a small set of trusted providers:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, StoreKit (subscriptions), push notifications, App Store.
  • Cloudflare — Workers and KV for backend logic including waitlist code redemption.
  • Render — signaling and relay server for peer-to-peer file routing.
  • Tally — waitlist email collection form (only for users who joined the waitlist).

Each provider is used only for its stated purpose and receives only the data strictly necessary to perform that function.

Data retention

  • Transfer metadata: up to 24 hours, then automatically deleted.
  • Account data: kept as long as your account exists. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Waitlist email: kept until you unsubscribe or delete your account.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Sign out of your Apple ID inside Poof (Settings → Account → Sign out Apple ID).
  • Reset all local data (Settings → Account → Reset all data).
  • Request full account deletion by emailing us. We will erase your server-side data within 30 days.
  • Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.

Children

Poof is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we will update the effective date above and, when appropriate, notify you in the app.
